## Short-Form Isn't One Thing Anymore

When TikTok launched, "short-form video" meant one thing: quick, casual, vertical clips. In 2026, short-form video is a **taxonomy** — different formats for different goals, audiences, and algorithms.

The creators dominating their niches understand exactly which format serves which purpose. Let's break them all down.

<Alert variant="info">
  <AlertTitle>The 60-Second Rule is Dead</AlertTitle>
  <AlertDescription>
    Platform algorithm updates in late 2025 showed that videos between 90 seconds and 3 minutes consistently outperform sub-60-second clips on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts. The "short" in short-form no longer means under a minute.
  </AlertDescription>
</Alert>

## The 8 Formats That Matter in 2026

<Tabs items={["Hook Clips", "Edu-Clips", "Story Loops", "React Clips", "Proof Clips", "List Videos", "POV Clips", "Trend Rides"]}>
  <Tab value="Hook Clips">
    ### The Hook Clip
    **Format:** 15–45 seconds | **Best platforms:** TikTok, Reels

    A single, punchy moment extracted from longer content. Usually starts mid-sentence ("...and that's when everything changed") to trigger immediate curiosity.

    **Best use:** Driving traffic back to long-form content. Pure top-of-funnel.

    **AI production:** Ideal for ClypAI's auto-detection — the AI is trained to find these exact hooks.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Edu-Clips">
    ### The Educational Clip
    **Format:** 60–180 seconds | **Best platforms:** YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn

    One concept, explained clearly and concisely. Often includes text overlays reinforcing key points. High save rates, strong SEO value.

    **Best use:** Establishing expertise. These get bookmarked and re-watched.

    **AI production:** Pull from tutorial content, webinars, or explainer sections of interviews.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Story Loops">
    ### The Story Loop
    **Format:** 30–90 seconds | **Best platforms:** TikTok, Reels

    Ends where it begins, creating a compulsive re-watch loop. Works especially well for transformation reveals, process videos, and "how it's made" content.

    **Best use:** Maximizing watch time and algorithm boost on TikTok.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="React Clips">
    ### The Reaction Clip
    **Format:** 45–120 seconds | **Best platforms:** TikTok, YouTube Shorts

    You react to someone else's content, a news moment, or a trending topic. High relevance + low production friction.

    **Best use:** Riding existing conversation momentum. Works best when the reaction is genuine and adds value.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Proof Clips">
    ### The Social Proof Clip
    **Format:** 30–90 seconds | **Best platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Shorts

    A testimonial, a result screenshot, a before/after, or a milestone announcement. Nothing converts like proof.

    **Best use:** Bottom-of-funnel content for audiences already considering your offer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="List Videos">
    ### The List Video
    **Format:** 60–180 seconds | **Best platforms:** All platforms

    "5 things I wish I knew before..." or "The 3 mistakes costing creators 80% of their reach." Listicles in video form. High completion rates.

    **Best use:** When you want to educate and entertain simultaneously.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="POV Clips">
    ### The POV Clip
    **Format:** 30–60 seconds | **Best platforms:** TikTok, Reels

    First-person framing ("POV: you just automated your entire content workflow") with text-driven storytelling. Requires almost zero production.

    **Best use:** Relatability-driven content that sparks comments.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Trend Rides">
    ### The Trend Ride
    **Format:** 15–30 seconds | **Best platforms:** TikTok

    Adapting your niche to a trending audio, format, or meme. Has a 72-hour relevance window — speed is everything.

    **Best use:** Discoverability and algorithmic reach in short bursts.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
