## The 5-Step ClypAI Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Upload Your Source Video

    Drag and drop your file directly into ClypAI, or connect your YouTube channel, Twitch VOD library, or podcast feed. We support all major formats: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, and MP3 for audio-only content.

    **Pro tip:** You can batch-upload multiple videos. ClypAI processes them in parallel, so 10 videos don't take 10 times as long.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Let the AI Analyze Your Content

    While you grab coffee, ClypAI's AI engine:

    - Transcribes every word with speaker diarization
    - Identifies emotional peaks and energy shifts
    - Detects natural start/end boundaries for clips
    - Scores each candidate clip by estimated engagement potential

    This takes 2–8 minutes depending on video length. No manual input required.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review Your Clip Candidates

    ClypAI presents a ranked list of clip candidates — usually 8–20 for an hour of content. Each shows:

    - A thumbnail and preview
    - The transcript excerpt
    - An AI-generated "why this moment" explanation
    - Suggested caption and title

    You can approve, reject, or trim any clip with a single click. Most creators approve 40–70% of AI suggestions without changes.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Customize: Captions, Reframing & Branding

    For each approved clip:

    - **Captions** are auto-generated, styled, and animated — edit any word in-line
    - **Smart Reframe** converts 16:9 horizontal video to 9:16 vertical automatically, keeping the subject centered
    - **Branding** lets you add your intro/outro, logo watermark, and color scheme from your saved Brand Kit
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Export for Every Platform

    One click exports platform-optimized versions for:

    - **TikTok** — 9:16, max 10 minutes, with captions burned in
    - **Instagram Reels** — 9:16, optimized bitrate
    - **YouTube Shorts** — 9:16 with Shorts metadata
    - **LinkedIn** — 1:1 or 16:9, professional format
    - **Twitter/X** — 2:20 max, compressed for fast load

    All exports happen simultaneously. Your whole batch is ready in under 5 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Questions

### How accurate is the AI clip selection?

The AI is trained on millions of high-performing short-form videos. It's not perfect — no AI is — but most creators find 4–6 genuinely strong clips per hour of source content, which is exactly what manual editors produce, in a fraction of the time.

### Can I train it on my style?

Yes. The more you use ClypAI and the more feedback you give (approvals, rejections, edits), the more the AI learns your preferences. Brand Kits also teach the AI your visual and tonal identity.
