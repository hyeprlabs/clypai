## Caption Designer

<Callout type="success" title="Most-Requested Feature — Now Shipping">
  Caption Designer was the #1 requested feature since our launch. Today it's live for all users.
</Callout>

The Caption Designer gives you full control over how your captions look—not just the text, but the typography, positioning, animation, and styling that make your content instantly recognizable.

### What's included:

- **Font selection** with 40+ premium fonts optimized for video readability
- **Color + stroke** controls for text, background fill, and outline weight
- **Animation presets** including word-by-word reveal, fade-in, and bounce
- **Positioning templates** for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and LinkedIn natively
- **Brand kit** — save your style once, apply to every clip automatically

Caption styles are saved per-project and can be set as account defaults. Once you set your brand kit, your captions are automatically styled on every new clip—no configuration required.

## Enhanced Clip Detection v2

Our AI model for identifying high-value moments has been significantly upgraded.

### What improved:

**Multi-speaker accuracy** — v2 handles two or more speakers significantly better. When two hosts are talking, the model now correctly attributes enthusiasm and emotional spikes to the right speaker rather than averaging across the audio channel.

**Technical content** — Clips from developer streams, coding tutorials, and educational content now surface more relevant moments. The model has been trained on a broader corpus that includes domain-specific delivery patterns.

**Silence-to-spike recognition** — The pause before a punchline is a strong signal for a clip's starting point. v2 is significantly better at recognizing and using this pattern.

**Benchmark improvements:**

| Metric | v1 | v2 | Improvement |
|--------|----|----|-------------|
| User acceptance rate | 67% | 84% | +25% |
| First-clip selection | 43% | 61% | +42% |
| Multi-speaker accuracy | 71% | 89% | +25% |
| Technical content quality | 58% | 79% | +36% |

## Platform Export Presets

Exporting to multiple platforms used to require manual configuration for each one. Not anymore.

Platform Export Presets are intelligent templates that know exactly what each platform needs:

    - **Audio:** Normalized to TikTok's preferred -14 LUFS

## Archive Import

Connect your YouTube channel, Twitch, or podcast RSS feed and ClypAI will automatically scan your back catalog for high-value clip candidates.

Your archives—months or years of recordings—are an untapped content library. Archive Import surfaces the best moments from your past that are worth rediscovering.

## Bug Fixes & Improvements

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Fixed: Caption sync drift on longer videos">
    Captions were occasionally drifting out of sync on videos longer than 45 minutes. This has been corrected with an updated sync algorithm that recalibrates at chapter boundaries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fixed: Smart reframe losing subject on rapid camera movement">
    Fast camera pans were causing the smart reframe to temporarily lose the primary subject. The tracking model now handles velocity changes more gracefully.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Improved: Export speed">
    Average export times have been reduced by 40% through infrastructure improvements. A 60-second clip now exports in under 8 seconds on average.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Improved: Mobile web experience">
    The review and approval flow is now fully functional on mobile browsers. Review your AI-identified clips from your phone, approve or reject, and export—without touching a desktop.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
