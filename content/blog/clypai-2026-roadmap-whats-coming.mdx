## A Message From Our Team

When we launched ClypAI, the vision was simple: eliminate the friction between creators and their audiences. Stop the timeline-scrubbing, the subtitle-typing, the reframing-by-hand that drains creative energy and produces diminishing returns.

We built the foundation. Now we're building the future.

This roadmap post is our commitment to transparency. These are the things we're building, why we're building them, and what we need from you to build them right.

## Q1 2026: Already Shipped

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Batch Processing** — Upload up to 20 videos simultaneously. ClypAI processes them in parallel, so your batch of 20 is ready in the same time as a single video.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Brand Kits v2** — Full logo, color scheme, font, and intro/outro management. Your brand applied automatically to every clip with zero repetition.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **LinkedIn Export Format** — Native 1:1 and 4:5 aspect ratio exports optimized for LinkedIn's feed algorithm, with the correct bitrate and metadata settings.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Transcript Editor** — Edit any word in your auto-generated transcript directly in-app before exporting captions. No more re-uploading for caption corrections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Q2 2026: In Development

### AI Platform Scheduler

Direct publishing integration with TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn — **from inside ClypAI**. Approve clips, write captions, and schedule them to publish at optimal times, all in one interface.

No more jumping between five different tools.

### Clip Performance Analytics

Connect your social accounts and see which of your ClypAI-generated clips are actually performing. We'll surface the patterns: which hook styles get the highest completion rates for *your* audience, which topics drive the most followers.

The AI learns what works for you specifically — and gets better at selecting clips accordingly.

### Voice Style Matching

<Callout type="info">
  Upload a "reference clip" — your best-performing short-form content — and ClypAI will train a style model on it. Future clip suggestions will be biased toward the patterns that made your reference clips successful.
</Callout>

## Q3 2026: On the Horizon

<Tabs items={["Agentic Workflows", "Collaboration Features", "API & Integrations"]}>
  <Tab value="Agentic Workflows">
    ### Full End-to-End Automation

    Our most ambitious feature: a workflow that runs entirely without manual review.

    **How it works:**
    1. Connect your podcast feed, YouTube channel, or stream archive
    2. Set your style preferences and quality threshold
    3. ClypAI monitors for new content automatically
    4. Clips are generated, reviewed against your quality threshold, and published on schedule

    For creators who want to step back from production entirely — this is for you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Collaboration Features">
    ### Team Workspaces

    Brands, agencies, and creator teams need multi-user workflows. Q3 brings:

    - **Team accounts** with role-based permissions (Admin, Editor, Viewer)
    - **Review queues** — submit clips for team approval before publishing
    - **Shared Brand Kits** — one brand identity applied consistently across all team members
    - **Activity logs** — full audit trail of who created, approved, and scheduled what
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="API & Integrations">
    ### Developer API

    A public API allowing technical creators and developers to integrate ClypAI's clip generation, captioning, and reframing into their own tools and workflows.

    Early access for developer accounts in Q3. Full GA release in Q4.

    **Integration partners confirmed:** Zapier, Make.com, Notion, and Airtable for workflow automation.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
